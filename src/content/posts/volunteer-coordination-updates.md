Friends of Healing Springs Natural Area, Inc. is building its volunteer program to engage community members in hands-on conservation at Healing Springs Natural Area. Here is the latest on how you can get involved.

## Upcoming Workdays

We are scheduling initial volunteer workdays for trail maintenance and site cleanup. Dates and registration details will be posted on our [Get Involved](/get-involved/) page as they are confirmed.

## What Volunteers Can Expect

- Guided stewardship activities appropriate for a range of skill levels
- Safety briefing and equipment overview at each event
- Opportunity to learn about local ecology and conservation practices
- Direct contribution to protecting a significant natural area

## How to Sign Up

Contact us through our [Contact page](/contact/) to express interest in volunteering. Include your availability and any relevant experience. We will follow up with details about upcoming opportunities.

## Safety & Preparation

Volunteers should wear sturdy closed-toe shoes, long pants, and weather-appropriate clothing. Bring water and sun protection. Tools and gloves will be provided at organized workdays.

We look forward to working alongside our community to care for this special place.

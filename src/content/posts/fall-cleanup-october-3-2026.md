Friends of Healing Springs Natural Area is hosting a fall cleanup and stewardship workday on **Saturday, October 3, from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM**, and we'd love to have you join us.

We'll meet at the **Healing Springs common-area pond** and spend the day helping clean up and care for Healing Springs Natural Area and surrounding areas.

This will be a broad cleanup and stewardship day rather than a single project. We'll work on a variety of tasks depending on what needs to be done and how many people show up.

The goal is simple: **help take care of Healing Springs Natural Area and leave the area better than we found it.**

Arkansas Natural Heritage Commission (ANHC) staff will be joining us. It's a great chance to spend some time together, learn more about Healing Springs Natural Area, and help with hands-on stewardship.

You don't have to stay all day. **Come for an hour, come for the morning, or stay all day—we'd love to have you.**

Anyone who wants to help is welcome. This is a community workday, not a formal volunteer requirement.

## Possible work

We'll keep the day's work flexible. Depending on conditions and how many people join us, we may:

* Do general cleanup and remove debris
* Pick up tires and other accumulated trash
* Trim vegetation and small branches
* Do light trail stewardship and maintenance
* Help with other small stewardship projects identified by Friends of Healing Springs Natural Area and ANHC

This is not a tree-removal event, and we are not asking volunteers to cut down large trees. Any larger vegetation work remains subject to ANHC requirements and approval.

## What to Bring

Come prepared to work outdoors for several hours.

Recommended:

* Work gloves
* Water and hydration
* Sturdy boots or work shoes
* Sunscreen
* Bug spray / insect repellent
* A hat and weather-appropriate clothing
* Tools for trimming small branches and vegetation, **if you have them and are comfortable using them**
* A riding mower with a utility trailer, **if you have one and it is appropriate for the workday**

Bringing tools or equipment is **optional**. Please don't bring anything you are uncomfortable operating. We are not asking anyone to bring chainsaws or other heavy equipment.

## RSVP

Please [let us know you're coming](#rsvp). An RSVP helps us plan the day and match people with the kinds of work they enjoy. Submitting an RSVP does **not** mean you have to stay for the entire event.

A [printable flyer](/flyers/fall-cleanup-october-3-2026/) is also available if you'd like to share the workday with a neighbor or community group.

🌿 Friends of Healing Springs Natural Area — Brand Kit
🧭 Brand Essence

Mission:
Protect and restore a spring-fed watershed ecosystem through conservation, education, and stewardship.

Personality:

grounded
scientific
calm
credible
field-oriented (not corporate)

Avoid:

playful tone
marketing language
hype or activism framing
decorative aesthetics

📐 Layout Principles
generous whitespace
grid-based structure
minimal shadows
soft borders only
no heavy UI decoration
image-first storytelling
🧩 UI Style Rules
Buttons
flat design
slightly rounded corners (6–10px)
muted color fills (no neon or high saturation)
Cards
light sandstone background
subtle border (stone gray)
no heavy drop shadows
Icons
line-based SVG icons only
consistent stroke width
no filled cartoon icons
📸 Photography Style Guide
Must emphasize:
water movement (highest priority)
stream + rock interaction
riparian zones
natural erosion processes (scientific framing)
volunteer stewardship (real work, not posed smiles)
Avoid:
overly staged group photos
suburban-looking lawns
stock imagery
over-saturated greens
🗺️ Future Compatibility Notes

This brand system is designed to expand into:

interactive watershed map (Leaflet.js)
project tracking system
grant reporting dashboards
Stripe donation integration
🧠 Final Principle

This brand is not “environmental decoration.”
It is a visual system for ecological stewardship credibility.
